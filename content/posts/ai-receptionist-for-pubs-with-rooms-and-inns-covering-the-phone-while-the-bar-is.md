## The two-in-one phone problem

In a hotel, the phone belongs to reception. In a pub with rooms, it belongs to whoever is nearest, and that person is usually holding a tray, a pan or a barrel. The same number gets asked about three different businesses:

- **Rooms:** "Do you have a double on Saturday?", "Do you take dogs?", "What time is check-in if I arrive after the bar closes?"
- **Food and drink:** "Can I book a table for six at 7pm?", "Do you do a Sunday roast?", "Is the kitchen open?"
- **Events:** "Can we hold a christening here?", "Is there a quiz night?", "Do you take wedding parties?"

Each type needs a different person. A room enquiry may wait until morning, a table enquiry may be gone in a minute, and an event enquiry needs a proper conversation. Rooms are also usually the smaller business, so a full-time receptionist is hard to justify.

## What an AI receptionist can do for the room side

**Answer routine room questions from a profile.** You paste your website link and the AI drafts a profile of rooms, hours and policies. Your team reviews it before anything goes live. For a pub: check-in times, parking, dogs, breakfast, whether the bar is the reception, and rates. Hanas quotes only the rates you enter. If no rate applies, it takes details and asks staff to call back.

**Take room-booking requests.** The AI collects the details and sends a request to staff. On Hanas, staff approve or decline each request by email, with a single approval button, or in a web dashboard. By default the AI does not confirm bookings and does not take payment or card details, and nothing is confirmed to the guest until staff say yes. These are current defaults. See [why staff approval matters](https://gethanas.com/blog/should-an-ai-confirm-hotel-bookings-why-staff-approval-matters).

**Pass on questions it cannot answer.** If a caller asks something the profile does not cover, Hanas says so, offers to pass it on and creates a callback request.

**Cover the moments nobody can answer.** Hanas states that it answers 24/7 and says it is an AI and that the call is recorded. For a pub, that means the Friday rush and the Sunday lunch peak.

**Speak to callers in other languages.** Hanas lists English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Russian and Arabic in its demo, and says it can switch to Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Chinese and Japanese during calls. It also says quality differs by language and that it does not guarantee any language. See [handling foreign-language callers](https://gethanas.com/blog/how-to-handle-foreign-language-callers-at-a-small-uk-hotel).

Most pubs with rooms have no PMS, and Hanas does not connect to one today (Mews, Cloudbeds, Little Hotelier, Guestline and eviivo are planned, no dates). See [AI receptionist without a PMS](https://gethanas.com/blog/ai-receptionist-without-a-pms-how-it-works-alongside-your-booking-system).

## What it cannot do, and where Hanas is weaker

This is the section that matters most for a pub.

**Events and functions.** Same position. A wedding enquiry needs a person. Have the AI take a name, number and note for a manager call back.

## Late arrivals and key handover

Pubs with rooms have a problem hotels with a 24-hour desk do not: the bar closes, and the person who knows the rooms goes home or goes upstairs. The guest who arrives at 11.30pm after a long drive needs a plan that does not depend on the phone.

Common approaches, as options and not recommendations:

- **Key safe with a code.** The code is sent to the guest after staff have approved the booking. Change the code regularly and between stays if your insurer or policy requires it.
- **Named person on call.** Give a mobile number to confirmed guests, not to every caller.
- **Fixed latest check-in time.** State it everywhere, including the AI's profile.

The AI can repeat the policy and pass an expected arrival time to staff. Your process, not the AI, must get the guest into the room.

## A practical set-up checklist

![Icon tiles for five set-up steps: split the calls, write the room facts, set late-arrival rules, name who approves, test with a trial](/blog/images/ai-receptionist-pub-with-rooms-inn-uk-pub-setup-checklist-tiles.png)

_Five steps before you go live_

1. **Decide what the AI is for.** Write down which call types it answers and which it passes on.
2. **Write the room facts.** Room types, rates you will quote, check-in times, parking, dogs, breakfast, accessibility, cancellation policy. Hanas drafts a profile from your website, then you correct it.
3. **Add only stable pub facts.** Opening and food service hours, yes. Specials and allergens, no.
4. **Write the late-arrival and key rules** into the profile word for word.
5. **Name who approves requests,** with a cover and a response target.
6. **Keep a manual route for food and events,** such as a booking form on your website.
7. **Check the data and legal side.** Hanas states that it says it is an AI and that calls are recorded. Transcripts and requests are stored in Ireland, deleted after 30 days by default, and its speech provider, ElevenLabs, processes calls in the US. Our [legal article](https://gethanas.com/blog/is-it-legal-to-use-an-ai-receptionist-at-a-uk-hotel) is general information, not legal advice.
8. **Run the trial on your own number.** Hanas states a 14-day or 60-minute trial, whichever comes first, on your own phone number. Test a table call and an event call.

## Who it suits, and who it does not

An AI receptionist is more likely to suit you if:

- most of your phone work is room-related and the rest can be passed to staff;
- calls land when you cannot answer, such as service, evenings and early mornings;
- you are comfortable with a person approving every room request;
- you get callers from outside the UK.

It is more likely to be the wrong choice if:

- most calls are table bookings and you want the AI to take and confirm them;
- you take events and functions through the phone and want instant answers on availability and price;
- you need payment or deposits on the call today, or a PMS, till or booking-platform connection now.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can an AI receptionist take table bookings for a pub?

Hanas's site does not list table bookings as a feature. Whether a call about a table is handled depends on the profile you write, and you should test it in the trial. If table bookings are your main need, a tool built for restaurant bookings may suit you better.

### Will the AI confirm a room booking while we are busy?

Not by default. Hanas sends each request to staff, and nothing is confirmed to the guest until they approve it.

### What about guests who arrive after the bar closes?

The AI can state your policy but cannot hand over a key. You need a key safe, a named person or a latest check-in time.

### Is it allowed to record calls?

Hanas says it tells callers it is an AI and that the call is recorded. The legal position depends on your use, so read our [legal article](https://gethanas.com/blog/is-it-legal-to-use-an-ai-receptionist-at-a-uk-hotel) and the Hanas [privacy notice](https://gethanas.com/privacy).
