This post is a format reference for anyone writing on the hanas.ai blog. It stays a **draft**, so it never appears on the site, in the feed or in the sitemap. Copy what you need from it, then delete what you do not.

The title above comes from the information block, so the body starts with a normal paragraph. Use *italics* sparingly and **bold** for the one phrase a reader must not miss.

## A section heading

Use `##` for sections and `###` for sub-sections. A post of 800 to 1,200 words usually needs three to five sections.

### A sub-section

Short paragraphs read better on a phone. Aim for two to four sentences each.

## Lists

An unordered list:

- Missed calls during check-in and check-out
- Calls that come in after the front desk closes
  - Late arrivals asking for the key code
  - Guests asking about breakfast times
- Booking enquiries that go to voicemail

A numbered list, for steps:

1. Tell us your website.
2. Check the profile we write for your hotel.
3. Add your rates and invite your team.

## Quotes

> A quote from a hotel owner goes here, with their permission and their name below it.
>
> Keep quotes short and real. Never invent one.

## Links

- A link to another page on our site: [how hanas.ai works](/#how).
- For prices, link to the [pricing section](/#pricing) instead of writing figures in the post.
- An external source, with the date you read it: [the ICO website](https://ico.org.uk/) (accessed 15 November 2026).
- An email link: [support@vibesapps.co](mailto:support@vibesapps.co).

Facts and claims about other companies always need a source and a date, as on our comparison pages.
