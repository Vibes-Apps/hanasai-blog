## Why independent hotels look for Conduit alternatives

The reasons we can support from Conduit's own pages are about fit and pricing, not quality. Conduit says it serves hotel groups, independent hotels, short-term-rental operators, resorts and villas, and much of what it describes (housekeeping boards, maintenance work orders, many short-term-rental PMS connections) suits larger or more complex operations. A four-room guest house that mainly wants night calls answered may be paying for a platform it will not use. Pricing is the second reason, as the next sections show.

## What Conduit does well

According to its own site and documentation, read today:

- **PMS context on calls.** Its independent hotels page says voice agents answer inbound calls "24/7 with full PMS context", naming Opera, Mews and Cloudbeds. Its integrations page also lists Apaleo and StayNTouch.
- **Several channels in one inbox.** Email, phone, SMS, WhatsApp and travel agency messaging.
- **Control over the AI.** Its documentation describes a copilot mode where staff approve replies, a testing sandbox, escalation rules and warm call transfers.
- **Security statement.** Its pricing page says it is SOC 2 Type II certified and that customer data is "never used to train shared models". We did not see the audit report.
- **Published credit rates.** It documents how voice minutes convert to credits.
- **Voice languages.** Its voice setup page lists English (including UK) and a long list of others.

## What a small property can and cannot model from Conduit's pages

The pricing page shows three per-unit plans and a flat plan, all "starting rates".

![Bar chart of Conduit's published starting prices per unit per month: Messaging $18, Guest $30, Full Suite $48](/blog/images/conduit-alternatives-independent-hotels-conduit-unit-prices.png)

_Conduit's starting prices per unit per month, as shown on its pricing page on 17 November 2026. Overwatch is custom-priced and Startup is a flat $899 a month for up to 25 units._

Voice AI is listed under the Messaging plan, the cheapest. Startup is $899 a month for up to 25 units with the full feature set and no implementation fee, according to the page. It says Conduit is "not free" and mentions no trial.

The voice cost is where the page stops. Conduit's documentation says credits are a unit of usage that reset each monthly billing cycle, and gives these voice rates:

| Activity | Credits (Conduit docs) |
| --- | --- |
| Regular voice minute | 1 per minute |
| AI voice minute | 8 per minute |

The same page says AI replies in the inbox do not use credits, and that when credits run low you can upgrade or continue into overage, charged on your next invoice. At those rates, 300 minutes of AI calls would be 2,400 credits. That arithmetic is ours; we cannot turn it into pounds.

![Flow showing how Conduit voice cost is worked out: plan price per unit, included credits (not stated), 8 credits per AI voice minute, overage charged on the next invoice (rate not stated)](/blog/images/conduit-alternatives-independent-hotels-conduit-credit-chain.png)

_What can and cannot be calculated from Conduit's public pages as of 17 November 2026._

What we could not find on the pages we read:

- The number of credits included in each plan.
- The price of a credit. Its changelog says the billing page shows "your current cost per credit", so it is visible after sign-up, but we did not find it publicly.
- The overage rate.
- What counts as a "unit" for a hotel. The pricing page says "unit" and mentions "50 listings", without saying how rooms count.
- Whether there is a contract term or minimum commitment.

So we cannot give a monthly figure for 300 minutes with Conduit. Ask for one in writing.

Two other points. Its documentation says the AI disclosure setting is off until you switch it on, and if it stays off you are responsible for telling people they are speaking to AI. And the pages we read do not state where call data is stored: its privacy notice names HostAI Labs, Inc. (doing business as Conduit) in San Francisco, with no hosting region.

## The alternatives in more detail

**D3x** publishes an "Independent & SMB" tier at €5 per room a month, with phone AI at €100 a month including 5 hours, then €12 per extra hour, billed monthly with no separate setup fee on that tier. It describes a typical rollout of two to three weeks, and contract length is confirmed on the demo call. See [Hanas vs D3x](https://gethanas.com/compare/d3x).

**Motel4** says setup takes about 15 minutes with no PMS connection. It shows a free tier of 30 minutes a month and paid tiers at $149, $299 and $599, with a 30-day money-back guarantee. It captures enquiries; payment on the call and data location are not stated.

**HiJiffy** shows starting prices per room per month (from £99 for Basic) without labelling which of two price sets is monthly, plus a one-off setup fee per five properties. Voice is listed under Pro, not Basic. Per its security page as read on 15 November, primary servers are in Ireland.

**Akia** claims booking and payment on the call. Prices are not published, and AI Voice is listed under the Max plan only. Its security page states SOC 2 Type II.

**Dextr AI** claims PMS-written bookings with a seven-day, demo-led onboarding and no published price. Its homepage says SOC 2 Type II is in progress. See [Hanas vs Dextr AI](https://gethanas.com/compare/dextr), and our wider shortlist, [7 best AI receptionists for UK small hotels](https://gethanas.com/blog/7-best-ai-receptionists-for-uk-small-hotels-and-b-bs-2026).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Conduit suitable for a small independent hotel?

It lists independent hotels among its customers. Whether it suits a very small property depends on the quote, because the voice cost depends on allowances not on the public pricing page. Ask for a worked example with your room count and call minutes.

### How much does Conduit cost per month?

According to its own pricing page, plans start at $18, $30 and $48 per unit a month, with Startup at $899 a month for up to 25 units. Voice is billed in credits at 8 credits per AI minute according to its documentation. A total for your hotel is not stated on the pages we read.

### Does Conduit take bookings or payment on calls?

Booking and payment on calls are not stated on the pages we read. Its hotel page describes spa treatment booking and says voice agents answer with PMS context. Confirm the exact booking and payment steps in a demo.
