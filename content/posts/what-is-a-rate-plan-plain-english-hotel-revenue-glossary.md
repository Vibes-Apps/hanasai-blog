A rate plan is a price for a room together with the rules that come with it: what is included (such as breakfast), whether the guest can cancel, and when they pay. The same room can have several rate plans, for example a flexible rate and a cheaper non-refundable one. For a small hotel, the useful idea is that a guest is not buying "a room at £X". They are buying a room under a set of conditions, and your website, your booking sites and your team all need to describe those conditions the same way. This glossary page explains the term, the common types, and how rate plans relate to room types, your PMS and your phone line.

## Rate plan in plain English

Think of two guests who book the same double room on the same night. One pays a lower price and cannot cancel. The other pays more and can cancel up to a set time before arrival. They are in the same room type, but on two different rate plans.

So a rate plan answers four questions:

- **How much?** The price, which can change by date, day of the week or season.
- **What is included?** Breakfast, parking, a late check-out, or nothing extra.
- **Can the guest cancel or change?** And what happens if they do.
- **When do they pay?** At booking, a deposit, or on arrival. How this works also depends on your booking tool and payment provider.

## Rate plan, room type and rate: the difference

These three words get mixed up.

| Term | What it means | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Room type** | The kind of room you sell | Double, twin, family room |
| **Rate plan** | The price and conditions you sell it under | Flexible, non-refundable, with breakfast |
| **Rate** | The price itself for a night or stay | The figure shown for a given date |

In most systems, you create room types first, then attach one or more rate plans to each. The price for each night sits inside the rate plan.

## Common types of rate plan

Names are not standard, so treat these as familiar patterns rather than fixed definitions.

![Table card of four common rate plan types: flexible, non-refundable, bed and breakfast, longer stay, with what each usually means](/blog/images/rp-types.png)

_Four common rate plan types and what they usually mean_

| Type | What it usually means | Worth checking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flexible** | Can be cancelled or changed up to a set time before arrival | The cut-off time and any fee |
| **Non-refundable** | Lower price, no refund if the guest cancels | That the guest is shown this clearly before they book |
| **Bed and breakfast** | Breakfast included in the price | What counts as breakfast and when it is served |
| **Longer stay** | A different price or condition for a minimum number of nights | The minimum nights and any date limits |
| **Advance purchase** | A price for booking a set time ahead | The booking window and cancellation terms |

You do not need all of these. A five-room guest house might run one or two. More plans mean more to keep consistent.

## Why consistency matters

Guests can reach your rates in several places: your own website, online travel agents, your phone line, and messages. If a rate plan says "free cancellation" on one and "non-refundable" on another, someone will be unhappy at check-in, and you will be the one explaining.

## A short checklist for a small hotel

1. Write each rate plan down in one sentence: price rule, inclusions, cancellation, payment.
2. Make sure the cancellation terms are shown to the guest before they book.
3. Check that the same plan reads the same on your website, your booking sites and in your PMS.
4. Make sure whoever answers the phone, including any automated service, has the list.
5. Review the list when you change a price or add an offer, and remove plans you no longer sell.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a rate plan in a hotel?

It is a price for a room plus the conditions attached: what is included, cancellation terms and payment terms.

### Is a rate plan the same as a room type?

No. A room type is the room (for example a double). A rate plan is the price and conditions it is sold under. One room type can have several rate plans.

### How many rate plans does a small hotel need?

Often one to three. Add a plan only if you will keep it consistent everywhere guests can book.

### What is a non-refundable rate?

A rate, usually lower, where the guest does not get a refund if they cancel. Make sure it is clearly shown before booking.

### Where do I set up rate plans?

Usually in your PMS or booking engine, and sometimes in a channel manager. Check which one holds the master list at your hotel.
