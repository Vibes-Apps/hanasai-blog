## How much does an AI phone receptionist cost a small hotel?

Published monthly prices for whole-property AI phone plans run from about $19 (CallSphere's entry plan, with usage billed on top) to $899 (Conduit's flat Startup plan). Per-unit and per-room prices start lower ($18 per unit at Conduit, €5 per room at D3x) but scale with property size. Among plans that bundle call minutes, entry prices are around £49 or $49 for 150 to 250 minutes. Several vendors publish in dollars or euros, and we do not convert them. Several hotel-focused vendors, including Dextr AI, Canary and Akia, did not show a price on the pages we could load on 15 November 2026. The honest answer for a 20-room hotel is therefore a range, and the real figure depends on call minutes, room count and whether you need your property management system (PMS) connected.

> **Key Takeaways**
>
> - Published monthly prices for whole-property plans run from about $19 to $899, depending on the product and what is included. Per-unit and per-room prices start lower but scale with property size, so the headline numbers are not comparable bundles.
> - Three price units are in use: per minute, per room or unit, and a flat monthly plan. Compare them using your own call minutes and room count, not the headline number.
> - Our workings put one hour of UK night cover at roughly £14.50 to £15.50 once employer costs are added: about £14.52 on average for a full-time employee at minimum wage, and about £15.34 for an extra hour at £13 pay. That is an estimate from government rates, not a quote.
> - Published prices often do not state whether VAT, PMS integration, setup, telephony or overage are included. Ask about each one.

## What a published price may leave out

The monthly figure is rarely the whole bill. Before you compare two vendors, ask each about:

- **VAT.** Hanas shows its price excluding VAT. Check how every other vendor states it.
- **Billing term.** My AI Front Desk shows $79 on annual billing and $99 monthly for the same plan.
- **Setup fees.** [HiJiffy](https://www.hijiffy.com/pricing) lists a one-off setup fee per five properties. [Conduit](https://www.conduit.ai/pricing) says its Startup plan has no implementation fee. [D3x](https://www.d3x.ai/pricing) says its Independent and SMB tier has none.
- **Contract length.** [D3x](https://www.d3x.ai/pricing) says contract length is confirmed on the demo call.
- **Overage.** Several usage-based plans in the table above charge extra after the included minutes.
- **PMS integration.** Some vendors place it in a higher tier. [HiJiffy](https://www.hijiffy.com/pricing), for example, lists PMS and CRM integration in its Premium plan.
- **Telephony.** How calls reach the AI (call forwarding, a new number) may carry separate telephone costs. The pricing pages we read do not say.

## What UK night cover costs, with workings

Staff cost is the benchmark most hotel owners have in mind. The inputs below come from GOV.UK. The output is our estimate, not a quote.

- **Minimum pay:** the National Living Wage for ages 21 and over is [£12.71 an hour from 1 April 2026](https://www.gov.uk/national-minimum-wage-rates).
- **Employer National Insurance:** [15% above £96 a week (£5,000 a year)](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) for the 2026 to 2027 tax year.
- **Employer pension:** [a 3% minimum on qualifying earnings](https://www.gov.uk/workplace-pensions/what-you-your-employer-and-the-government-pay), which GOV.UK sets between £6,240 and £50,270 a year.

**Our workings.** Take a full-time employee on exactly the National Living Wage, working 37.5 hours a week for 52 weeks (1,950 paid hours, with holiday inside those hours).

- Gross pay: £12.71 × 1,950 = £24,784.50
- Employer NI: 15% × (£24,784.50 − £5,000) = £2,967.68
- Employer pension: 3% × (£24,784.50 − £6,240) = £556.34
- Total: £28,308.52, or about £14.52 for each paid hour

For an extra hour worked by someone already above the NI and pension thresholds, the cost is about £12.71 × 1.18 = £15.00, and at £13 an hour it is £13 × 1.18 = £15.34. The £14.52 figure is the full-time average at minimum wage, and £15.00 to £15.34 is the cost of an extra hour at £12.71 to £13. We therefore use a range of roughly £14.50 to £15.50 an hour for a night role. Pay above £13 raises it.

**One overnight shift every night.** Eight hours a night over 365 nights is 2,920 hours. At £14.52 to £15.34 an hour (the top of that range assumes £13 an hour pay) that is roughly £42,000 to £45,000 a year. This leaves out holiday and sickness cover, recruitment, training and tips. It also leaves out the Employment Allowance, which [GOV.UK sets at £10,500](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027) for 2026 to 2027 and which can offset employer National Insurance for eligible small employers. As a loose cross-check on pay, the job-ad aggregator Jooble showed an average of [£12.55 an hour for night porter adverts](https://uk.jooble.org/salary/night-porter) on 11 November 2026. That is advertised pay, not payroll data, and it is slightly below the £12.71 adult minimum, which may reflect adverts for younger workers (the page does not say), so we do not rely on it.

An AI receptionist is not a like-for-like swap for that role. A night porter handles guests in the building, security and emergencies. An AI receptionist answers calls. The fairer comparison is call cover, not a full night shift.

## When an AI receptionist is not the cheaper option

- **Very few calls.** If you miss only a handful of calls a month, a voicemail greeting and a call-back habit may cost less. [Count your own missed calls for a week](https://gethanas.com/blog/missed-calls-small-hotel-cost/) before you decide.
- **You need PMS-written bookings.** Some products that connect to your PMS publish no price, such as Dextr AI and Akia on the pages we read.
- **You need a human voice for complex enquiries.** Weddings, group bookings and unusual requests may suit a person better.
- **Call volume is high.** Usage-based plans scale with minutes, so a busy line can cost more than a flat plan.

## How to get a real quote

Ask each vendor the same five questions and write down the answers:

1. What is the total monthly cost for my room count and an estimate of my call minutes, including VAT?
2. What is the overage rate, and is it per minute or per call?
3. Are there setup, integration or telephony costs, and how long is the contract?
4. What happens at the end of included minutes: do calls stop, or do they keep being answered at the overage rate?
5. Can I cancel monthly?

## Frequently asked questions

### Is an AI receptionist cheaper than a night porter?

On published prices, usually yes for call cover alone. Our estimate for one overnight shift every night is roughly £42,000 to £45,000 a year before holiday cover. This is not a like-for-like comparison: an AI answers calls only, so it does not replace the other things a night porter does.
