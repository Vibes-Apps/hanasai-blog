A property management system, or PMS, is the software a hotel uses to run its day-to-day operations: it holds the reservations, the room inventory and the guest records, and it is where the front desk checks guests in and out. This guide explains what a PMS typically does, what it often does not, and what to check when you want other tools, such as an AI receptionist, to work alongside it.

## What a PMS does

Functions vary by product, but a PMS commonly covers:

- **Reservations.** Holding each booking: dates, room, guest details, rate and notes.
- **Availability.** Knowing which rooms are free on which nights.
- **Check-in and check-out.** The front-desk workflow, including room assignment.
- **Guest records.** Names, contact details, preferences and stay history.
- **Billing.** Charges, invoices and, in some products, payments.
- **Housekeeping.** Room status, so the team knows what needs cleaning.
- **Reports.** Occupancy and revenue summaries.

Some PMS products add extras, such as messaging, upselling or accounting links. Whether you need them depends on your hotel.

## What a PMS usually does not do

It helps to be clear about the gaps:

- **Answer the phone.** A PMS stores bookings; it does not talk to callers. A person or another tool does that.
- **Sell rooms to the public.** That is the job of a booking engine on your website, which feeds the PMS. See our post on the [difference between a booking engine, a channel manager and a PMS](https://gethanas.com/blog/booking-engine-vs-channel-manager-vs-pms-what-is-the-difference).
- **Manage every sales channel.** Pushing availability to online travel agents is usually done by a channel manager, though some PMS products include one.

## Do small hotels need a PMS?

Not always. A very small guest house with a handful of rooms may run on a diary or a spreadsheet and be perfectly happy. A PMS tends to earn its place when:

- Several people need the same live view of availability.
- You sell through more than one channel and want to avoid double bookings.
- You need invoices, reports or housekeeping lists without extra effort.

If you are choosing one, ask vendors for their own current pricing and feature pages and read them on the day. We do not compare PMS products in this post.

## What "integration" means

When a product says it integrates with your PMS, the details matter. Ask:

1. **Which direction does data flow?** Can the tool read availability, write bookings, or both?
2. **Is it live or batched?** Real-time sync and a nightly export are very different.
3. **Who sets it up?** The vendor, the PMS provider or you.
4. **What does it cost, and who charges it?** Some PMS vendors charge for access to their connection.
5. **What happens if the link fails?** A fallback matters more than a feature list.

## Working alongside a PMS without a connection

A lack of integration is not necessarily a blocker. Many small hotels use a simple hand-off: the tool takes a request, sends it by email or shows it in a dashboard, and a team member enters the booking in the PMS. That is slower than a live link but easy to run and easy to check.

## A short checklist before you connect anything

1. List what lives in your PMS today and who updates it.
2. Decide which tasks you want automated and which must stay manual.
3. Ask each tool what it reads from and writes to the PMS.
4. Agree who enters data when there is no live link.
5. Test with a few real bookings before relying on it.
6. Keep a note of the hand-off steps for new staff.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a PMS the same as a booking engine?

No. A booking engine takes bookings from guests online; a PMS manages the hotel's operations. Many products include both, but they are different jobs.

### Is a PMS the same as a channel manager?

No. A channel manager keeps availability and rates in sync across online travel agents and other channels. See our [comparison post](https://gethanas.com/blog/booking-engine-vs-channel-manager-vs-pms-what-is-the-difference).

### Can I run a hotel without a PMS?

Many very small properties do, using a diary or spreadsheet. Growth, extra channels and staff turnover are the usual reasons to move to one.

### Do AI receptionists need a PMS?

Not necessarily. Some read availability from a PMS; others work from rates you enter and pass requests to your team. Ask each vendor which it does today, not which it plans to do.

### What does a PMS cost?

It varies by product and changes over time. Check each vendor's own pricing page.
