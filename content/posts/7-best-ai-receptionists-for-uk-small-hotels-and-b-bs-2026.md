Motel4 also publishes tier prices, but not the minutes included in each tier. If you need bookings written into your property management system (PMS), D3x says its phone AI looks up and confirms changes to reservations in your PMS (whether it creates new bookings is not stated). Dextr AI and Akia claim to book on the call: Dextr AI shows no price and a demo-led setup, and Akia sends module pricing to sales, on the pages we read. The choice comes down to four of the questions below: whether the AI confirms bookings or only takes requests, whether it connects to your PMS, whether the price is public, and where call data is stored.\n\n**Key Takeaways**\n\n- Hanas and D3x publish prices that are simple to model for phone calls. Motel4, Conduit and HiJiffy publish prices too, but a small property cannot yet work out the voice cost from the pages alone. Dextr AI and Akia did not show a price on the pages we could load.\n- "Books on the call" means different things. Some products write a reservation into your PMS. Others capture an enquiry or a request for staff to follow up.\n- Among these seven, only HiJiffy (primary servers in Ireland) and Hanas (transcripts stored in Ireland) stated where data is stored on the pages we checked. Ask every vendor before you sign.\n- Every feature of the other six products below is the vendor's own claim. Then check the price unit (per room, per minute or per property), where call data is stored, how the vendor sets up your hotel, and what happens when the AI cannot help and how staff are told. The price, setup and data-location checks match questions we asked of every vendor above; how staff are told when the AI cannot help is not covered in the entries, so ask each vendor directly.\n\n### **Do I need PMS integration?**\n\nNot necessarily. A request-based AI such as Hanas or an enquiry-capture tool such as Motel4 works alongside your PMS: staff enter confirmed bookings themselves. PMS integration saves staff time. The vendors that emphasise it, such as Dextr AI and Akia, route pricing through a demo or sales conversation on the pages we read, and D3x describes a demo-led setup, though it publishes a per-room price.\n\n### **Does an AI receptionist replace night staff?**\n\nNot entirely. It answers calls and takes details. It does not handle in-person guests, security or on-site emergencies. Treat it as call cover, not a replacement for every night duty.\n\n### **How often is this list updated?**\n\nWe plan to re-check prices and features at least every 90 days and update the date at the top.
